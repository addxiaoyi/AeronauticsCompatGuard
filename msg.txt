Register AttachmentTypeSerializerAccessor: players could not log in

A real server crashed on player join with:
  IllegalClassLoadError: Illegal classload request for accessor mixin
  dev.aeronautics.compatguard.server.mixin.AttachmentTypeSerializerAccessor. The mixin is missing
  from aeronauticscompatguard.mixins.json which owns package
  dev.aeronautics.compatguard.server.mixin.* and the mixin has not been applied.
  at AttachmentHolder.localvar$enj000$aeronauticscompatguard$aeronauticsCompatGuard$stripUnknownAttachments
  at AttachmentHolder.deserializeAttachments -> Entity.load -> PlayerList.placeNewPlayer
ending in 'lost connection: Invalid player data' and 'Couldn't place player in world'.

UnknownAttachmentDataMixin casts to AttachmentTypeSerializerAccessor, but that accessor was never
listed in aeronauticscompatguard.mixins.json, so Mixin refuses to load it and the cast fails on the
first entity that carries attachments -- i.e. every joining player.

Fixed by adding the accessor to the mixins array. Verified on NeoForge 21.1.250: the log now shows
'Mixing AttachmentTypeSerializerAccessor ... into net.neoforged.neoforge.attachment.AttachmentType'
and /acg mixins still reports applied=6 missing=0.
