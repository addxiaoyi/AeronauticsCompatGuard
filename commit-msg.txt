Inventory other mods' mixins so overlaps are visible at runtime

Mixin exposes no query for foreign injection points, but the inputs are on disk: mod jars list
their mixin configs, configs list mixin classes, and the @Mixin/@Inject/@ModifyReturnValue
annotations are runtime-retained. MixinInventory scans mod files and the classpath on a daemon
thread and reports, for each of our 7 targets, which mod hits the same method and whether it
runs before or after our fixed priority of 900 (/acg conflicts, plus a startup log).

Enumeration verified live: 2 jars, 13 mixin classes, 48 ms, no overlap on a dev server that
exposes no third-party mixins. Matching/ordering extracted into ConflictMatcher (pure Java) and
verified by a 13-assertion harness: before/same/after ordering, class-wide overlaps, and exclusion
of a different method or class. The first run of that harness failed on my own wrong expected
value (3 vs 4), not on the code.
