Fix the moved-too-quickly snap-back for riding players

The supplied log had one 'moved too quickly' line. Its bytecode branch in handleMovePlayer ends
in teleport(player.getX(), getY(), getZ(), yRot, xRot) followed by return, so the server snaps the
player back and drops the rest of the movement packet. RidingMovementCorrectionMixin redirects that
single teleport call and skips it when the player is a passenger; handleMoveVehicle still validates
the vehicle's own movement.

Runtime verified: /acg mixins reports minecraft.riding_movement_correction -> APPLIED
(applied=5 missing=0 declared=8), /acg selftest still 11/11, no exceptions in the boot log.

The log's other signals are documented rather than 'fixed': the 800 removed-entity warnings are
already covered by RemovedEntityPairingMixin (bytecode shows vanilla sends after logging), and the
AllTheLeaks retention (32 ServerPlayer / 2276 LevelChunk / +1098MB) is attributed by /acg leaks but
not freed, because this mod cannot free what another mod holds.
