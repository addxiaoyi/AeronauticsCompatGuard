Make the guard pay for itself: config gates, O(1) ledger lookup, journal writes

Measured first with /acg bench on a real server, then fixed what the numbers showed:

- reserve() cost 4433 us/op because every transition rewrote the whole ledger
  (Gson serialise + atomic move on Windows). It now appends one journal line through a
  kept-open writer: 50.3 us/op (~88x faster). The snapshot is rebuilt every 256 entries
  and on shutdown.
- The transaction hot path copied the entire ledger to find one reservation
  (reservations() -> List.copyOf). Added CompatibilityServerState#reservation(UUID):
  12.6 ns/op vs 2391.6 ns/op for the copy at n=200 (~190x), used by InventoryTransaction
  and FluidTransaction.
- Entity churn tracking and chunk retention probes now default off behind
  config/aeronauticscompatguard-common.toml, so a pack installing this for the safety
  fixes does not pay for diagnostics it never reads.
- The startup index hash budget is configurable.

Verified: /acg selftest still 11/11, and a seeded journal entry replays into the ledger
on boot (status reservations=1) then compacts into the snapshot on stop.
